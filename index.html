01 / The klub

Make the question
interesting.

We bring together students who want to understand how quantitative ideas behave when they meet real data.

Some members arrive with a strategy idea. Others arrive with a mathematical concept, a programming question, or simply a curiosity about markets. We share the tools, ask better questions, and learn by trying things ourselves.
