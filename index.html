How it works

From a question to something real.

01
Ideation
A paper, a hunch, a dataset, or something a lecture left unfinished. Narrow it until it names what would settle it.
02
Learning process
Read it, code it, simulate it, argue about it. Most of the time goes here, and that is the point of the club.
03
Output
A finding, a tool, a write-up, or a clear negative result. All four count as finished work.
