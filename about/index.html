SDU · Odense New klub, first members

A club for people who want to know why.

Kwant Klubben is an independent student club at the University of Southern Denmark exploring quantitative finance through code, data, mathematics, and discussion.

01 / What it is

New, small, and building.

The klub is new. It is currently founded and run by one student, and the first thing it is building is a member community — people who want somewhere to take a quantitative question seriously outside a course deadline.

The work is research and simulation: reading, coding, testing ideas against data, and working out what the result actually supports. The klub does not execute trades and makes no claim to a research track record. Those could come later, honestly earned. There is no reason to pretend about them now.

02 / The process, properly

What a project actually looks like.

The landing page has the short version. Here is what the three steps really involve, because the middle one is where almost all the time goes and it is the reason to be in a club rather than doing this alone.

01 — IDEATION

Make it falsifiable

Most interesting questions start too vague to answer. The work here is narrowing one until it names what evidence would settle it — which is usually also the moment you find out whether you actually care about it.

02 — LEARNING PROCESS

Where the time goes

Finding data, cleaning it, writing the thing, discovering the first version answered a different question, and rewriting. You will learn more here than the answer is worth, which is the actual reason the klub exists.

03 — OUTPUT

Say what it supports

Write down what you did, what came out, and what it does and does not license you to conclude. That last part is the hard one, and it is the difference between a result and a number.

03 / How we use AI

Fast tools, human ownership.

AI is useful for setup, data plumbing, debugging, explanations and first drafts, and the klub uses it openly for all of that. It removes the tedious part of getting to the interesting part.

What it does not do is own the work. You choose the question, read the sources, understand the code that matters, check the numbers, and decide what the result means. If you cannot explain why an answer is right, it is not yet your answer.

04 / Joining

All levels, genuinely.

Who it's for

Open to students from economics, mathematics, computer science and engineering, whatever amount of finance and programming you arrive with. Both ends of that range are wanted, and it will be a better klub when both are in the room.

What's expected

Participation. Turn up, take something on, tell people what you found. Around exams and when life gets busy that dips — everyone here is a student and that is understood.

How to apply

One short form, open continuously. It asks about your background so we can point you somewhere useful, not to filter you out. No CV, no motivation essay, no proof of work. We get in touch with everyone who applies.

Bring a question.

Applications are open continuously, mainly to students at SDU. Bring the question and the klub will help you get it somewhere.
