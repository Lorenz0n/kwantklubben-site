Kwant Klubben connects ambitious students with people and organizations doing quantitative work in the real world.

01 / Collaboration

Bring a problem,
a perspective,
or a door in.

The best partnerships do not feel like advertising. They give students a closer look at how quantitative finance is actually practiced.

Partners can help shape workshops, share opportunities, mentor students, propose research questions, host company visits, or support events and prizes. In return, you get a direct channel to students who are deliberately learning the tools of quantitative work.

02 / Ways to work together
01

Workshops & talks

Share a method, a dataset, a case, or an honest account of what the textbooks leave out.

02

Mentorship

Help students turn curiosity into stronger questions, projects, and professional direction.

03

Opportunities

Bring internships, student roles, events, and local quantitative work into the community.

04

Co-designed events

Build a hackathon, research challenge, or social event around a problem worth exploring.
