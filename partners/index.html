Partners Collaboration, not sponsorship

Work with the club.

We're interested in people and organisations who want to do something with a room full of students who chose to spend their own evenings on quantitative finance.

01 / What we're looking for

Come and do something.

Anything here is worth a conversation, and we'd rather design it with you than hand you a package.

Talks and workshops

An evening with the club: a technical talk, a dataset to attack together, or an honest account of what a job in this field is actually like day to day.

workshoptalk

Mentorship

Someone willing to look at what a member is building and tell them where it's weak. A small amount of this goes a long way at this stage.

mentoring

Events, designed together

A case evening, a competition, a joint session with another club or department. We're open about what we can realistically fill a room for.

eventsco-design
02 / What we can offer

A new club, said plainly.

Kwant Klubben is new. It's building its first member community, and we're not going to quote you an audience size we can't yet stand behind.

What we can offer is direct contact with students at SDU who are self-selected for this - people from economics, mathematics, computer science and engineering who chose to spend unpaid time on quantitative work - and a public project library you can read before you ever speak to any of them.

If your interest is meeting people who'll graduate into quantitative roles, that's a perfectly good reason to talk to us, and we'd rather you said so.

03 / Get in touch

Let's talk.

No deck required. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll tell you honestly whether the club can deliver it yet.
