01 / What we are looking for

Come and
do something.

Anything on this list is worth a conversation, and we would rather design it with you than hand you a package.

01

Talks and workshops

An evening with the klub: a technical talk, a dataset to attack together, or an honest account of what a job in this field is actually like day to day.

02

Mentorship

Someone willing to look at what a member is building and tell them where it is weak. A small amount of this goes a long way at this stage.

03

Events, designed together

A case evening, a competition, a joint session with another club or department. We are open about what we can realistically fill a room for.

02 / What we can offer

A new club,
said plainly.

Kwant Klubben is new. It is building its first member community, and we are not going to quote you an audience size we cannot yet stand behind.

What we can offer is direct contact with students at SDU who are self-selected for this — people from economics, mathematics, computer science and engineering who chose to spend unpaid time on quantitative work — and a public project library you can read before you ever speak to any of them.

If your interest is meeting people who will graduate into quantitative roles, that is a perfectly good reason to talk to us, and we would rather you said so.
