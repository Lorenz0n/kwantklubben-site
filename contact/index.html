01 / Before you write

Two kinds
of message.

If you are a student who wants to join, you do not need to email first — the application form is the fastest route, it is open continuously, and it goes to the same place.

If you are anyone else — a company, a lecturer, another club, someone with a dataset or a talk in mind — email is the right door, and it reaches a person rather than a queue.

There is no contact form on this site and nothing here collects your details. Email or LinkedIn is the whole of it.

02 / Join

Bring a
question.

Applications are open continuously, mainly to students at SDU. All levels are welcome — we ask about your background so we can point you somewhere useful, not to filter you out.
